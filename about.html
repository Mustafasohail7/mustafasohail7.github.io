Philosophy

My life philosophy revolves around three pillars: minimalism, curiosity and persistence. The same philosophy applies to my work as well. Minimalism, to me, means focusing on the problem before the solution. Modern software often accumulates layers of features and abstractions that obscure its original purpose. I prefer to understand the simplest possible solution first and only add complexity when it serves a clear purpose.

This is why I value understanding tools at their lowest practical level. Burp Suite is incredibly useful, but many of its core functions can be replicated with curl and a bit of scripting. The goal is not to avoid tools, it is to understand what they are doing underneath. Understanding the fundamentals keeps me tool-agnostic and UI-independent.

Curiosity is what drives me to keep digging deeper. I enjoy understanding why things work, how they fail, and what assumptions exist beneath the surface. Persistence is what turns curiosity into results. It's just as simple as putting in the work everyday and believing in delayed gratification.
